# Mechanical

This directory contains the enclosure and mechanical design for the Compact Real-Time Audio Processor.

Mechanical design will be developed alongside the PCB and user-interface layout so that connectors, controls, the display, and internal electronics fit together as a complete system.

---

## Mechanical Design Considerations

The enclosure design should account for:

- PCB mounting and support
- Audio and power connector access
- Rotary encoder and button placement
- Display position and visibility
- Speaker mounting, if retained in the final design
- Cable routing and strain relief
- Ventilation and thermal management
- Assembly and disassembly
- Access for debugging and testing
- Clearances between the PCB, enclosure, connectors, and controls

The final enclosure dimensions, materials, mounting hardware, and manufacturing method have not yet been selected.

---

## Design Files

Mechanical design files should be added as the enclosure develops.

These may eventually include:

- Native CAD files
- Exported STEP files
- Dimensioned drawings
- 3D-printable files
- Assembly drawings or instructions

Keep editable source files when practical so the enclosure can be modified as the PCB and hardware change.

---

## Validation

Before finalizing the enclosure, verify:

- PCB fit and mounting-hole alignment
- Connector accessibility
- Control spacing and usability
- Display visibility
- Speaker clearance and mounting, if applicable
- Cable clearance and strain relief
- Assembly access
- Thermal and ventilation needs

CAD fit checks should be followed by physical verification using the actual hardware before the enclosure is considered final.
